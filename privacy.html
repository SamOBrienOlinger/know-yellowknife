Privacy

A minimal-data prototype

Quiz storage

The quiz stores only your best score and a data-schema version in your browser’s local storage. It does not create an account or send that score to a server. Clear this site’s browser data to remove it.

Translation

The Translate control does not load third-party translation code when you open a page. Google Translate is contacted only when you choose a supported machine-translation language, and the translated copy opens in a new tab. Google may then process the public page content and set cookies under its own privacy terms. The Inuktitut and Tłı̨chǫ options open official Government of the Northwest Territories language-resource pages instead of sending this page for machine translation. This site does not store your language choice.

Contact demonstration

The private-review contact form validates entries in your browser but does not transmit or store them. A production privacy notice must be added when a real form service is configured.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not include analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies of its own. The optional Google Translate service may use cookies after you choose a language.

External links

Source links open third-party websites governed by their own privacy practices.
