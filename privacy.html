Your information and choices

You can read the site and play the quiz without an account, saved scores or Google Translate. Optional features are off until you choose them. Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the page to change or withdraw permission.

Browsing and hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this static website. Your browser sends connection information such as an IP address, browser request and requested address to deliver and secure the pages. Hosting records are not the same as an optional advertising tracker. GitHub explains its processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements in its privacy statement. Browser settings here cannot delete provider-held records.

Browser storage

know-yellowknife:privacy:v1 — necessary choice record
Stores the time, notice version and your two permissions when you make a choice. It is not an advertising identifier and is not uploaded to the project operator. Choices are valid for 180 days and requested again when the site next detects expiry. Local storage does not physically delete itself while the browser is closed.
kykQuizHighScore and kykQuizSchema — optional score saving
Store only the best quiz score and its data-format version after you allow score-saving. They are not sent to a server. Withdrawal, Clear saved quiz score, or a subsequent check finding expired or invalid consent removes these keys. Without permission the quiz works, but the current-visit best score is lost on reload.

These are local-storage entries, not tracking cookies. Earlier versions used these score names without consent. The updated controls do not read that score without permission and clear it when there is no valid choice. Other projects hosted on the same GitHub Pages origin may have their own data; clearing all origin data can affect them too.

Optional translation

No Google translation widget is loaded automatically. With JavaScript enabled, selecting a Google Translate link without permission opens the settings first. Allowing translation and choosing a language opens a translated copy on Google in a new tab. Google receives the public page address/content and connection information, including your IP address, and may use cookies under its own privacy notice. Translation can contain errors.

Inuktitut and Tłı̨chǫ options link to official Government of the Northwest Territories language resources rather than sending this page for machine translation. Ordinary external links have the destination's privacy rules. Browser commands can also open external links directly.

Withdrawing translation permission here stops future consent-controlled activations on this site; it cannot close existing Google tabs, reverse prior disclosures or erase cookies on Google's domain.

Contact demonstration and real email

The demonstration contact form validates entries in your browser but does not transmit or store them. Do not treat its demonstration confirmation as delivery of a message. Use the email address above for a real enquiry. If a real form service is introduced, its data flow and notice must be reviewed before activation.

Email you choose to send contains your address, message and attachments and is handled through the email services involved. It is used to reply and manage the enquiry, based on the legitimate interest in responding to people who contact the project. Retention should be limited to resolving the enquiry, maintaining a necessary record or meeting an applicable legal requirement; ask for the position for a particular message. Do not include unnecessary sensitive details. Contact does not automatically subscribe you to marketing.

External resources and tracking

Source links lead to independent websites. An externally hosted image or other resource, where used, receives connection data when requested by your browser. This privacy update introduces no analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. Any new embedded or tracking service needs its own review and, where required, must remain blocked until consent.
