Accessibility

Designed to work for more people

Know Yellowknife aims to make local learning clear, usable and welcoming across devices and access needs.

Our accessibility target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA and apply Level AAA improvements where practical.

Features included

  • Keyboard navigation, a skip link and visible focus indicators
  • Screen-reader-friendly landmarks, labels and status messages
  • Text resizing and page zoom without loss of content
  • Mobile layouts that reflow without sideways page scrolling
  • Large touch targets and clear labels for controls
  • Support for reduced motion, increased contrast and forced-colour preferences
  • Answer feedback that does not rely on colour alone
  • Clearly labelled, automated convenience translations into French, Filipino (Tagalog) and Punjabi—the three most-spoken home languages after English in Yellowknife’s 2021 Census profile
  • An automated Inuktut (Inuktitut) version plus official GNWT resources for Tłı̨chǫ and Dene Kǝdǝ́ (North Slavey)
  • BCP 47 language metadata so browsers and assistive technologies can identify translated content correctly

Translation

The Translate control opens a Google-generated convenience translation in a new tab for French, Filipino (Tagalog), Punjabi or Inuktut. None of these translations has been reviewed by a fluent speaker, and none is described as official or authoritative. The English content remains the reference version.

The first three choices and the Indigenous-language ordering are informed by the 2021 Census Profile for Yellowknife (opens in a new tab). The Government of the Northwest Territories identifies Inuktitut and Tłı̨chǫ as Yellowknife’s official community Indigenous languages (opens in a new tab) and maintains an Indigenous-language interpreter and translator list (opens in a new tab). Complete Tłı̨chǫ or Dene Kǝdǝ́ editions will not be presented as translations until qualified fluent speakers translate and independently review the site content.

Known limits

Automated checks and keyboard testing cannot reproduce every assistive technology or individual access need. Third-party websites linked from this resource have their own accessibility arrangements.

Report a problem

If something is difficult to read, navigate or understand, please describe the page and the problem through the contact and corrections form. The current form is a private-review demonstration and does not transmit messages.
