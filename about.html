About the project

Learning that strengthens belonging

Know Yellowknife is an independent educational prototype created by Allies in Action.

Our mission

Know Yellowknife makes credible introductory learning about the city easier to discover and more engaging to explore. It combines concise reading with a replayable quiz, drawing on play-based and experiential-learning principles.

Editorial approach

The MVP uses publicly available information from Indigenous organizations and official public bodies. It has not undergone formal community review and does not speak on behalf of the City, Indigenous governments or community organizations. Each learning card and quiz explanation links to its source so readers can continue with authoritative material.

We prioritize an organization’s own website when describing its identity, history and current work. Regional NWT information is labelled separately from Yellowknife-local information. Corrections are welcome through the demonstration contact page.

Project origins

Know Yellowknife evolved from AllyIndex (opens in a new tab), created by the Allies in Action team during the Code Institute July 2024 hackathon. This MVP reuses the idea of learning through a quiz while replacing the original subject matter and rebuilding its interface and content structure for Yellowknife.
